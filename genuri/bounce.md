## 📋 Fișa Genului

| Atribut | Valoare |
|---------|---------|
| **BPM** | 150-165 (tipic: 155-160) |
| **Key tipic** | Cm, Fm, Gm, Bbm |
| **Energie** | ⭐⭐⭐⭐⭐ (MAXIMUM) |
| **Structură** | Intro → build → big drop → breakdown → massive drop → outro |
| **Instrumente** | Big kicks, offbeat bass, donk, siren synths, vocal chops |
| **Vibe** | Party hard, maxim energie, frenetic |

---

## 🔊 Sub-genuri

| Sub-gen | BPM | Caracter |
|---------|-----|---------|
| **Melbourne Bounce** | 128-135 | Mai lent, big room feel |
| **Scouse House** | 150-160 | Classic donk sound |
| **Hard Bounce** | 155-165 | Agresiv, hard kicks |
| **Psybounce** | 150-155 | Psy elements + bounce |

---

## 🎛️ Tehnici de Mixaj

| Tehnică | Cum | Când |
|---------|-----|------|
| **Quick blend** | 8-16 bars (energetic, nu lung) | Standard |
| **Drop swap** | Sync ambele drops exact | Impact maxim |
| **Volume smash** | Full volume pe drop (no subtlety) | Peak time |
| **Rewind** | Pull back → drop din nou | Crowd interaction |

## 🎹 Cu Circuit Tracks

- **Synth 1:** Big lead synth (saw wave, detuned)
- **Synth 2:** Offbeat bass (bounce characteristic)
- **Drums:** Hard kicks, snare rolls rapide
- **Truc:** Build-up-uri cu snare roll crescendo → drop!

---

## ⚠️ Note

- Bounce e gen de **peak time** — nu deschide seara cu bounce
- Folosește bounce ca **climax** al setului
- 15-20 minute de bounce = suficient (altfel obosești publicul)

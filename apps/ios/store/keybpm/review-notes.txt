MixAI Key & BPM detects the musical key and tempo of the music playing near the device. No account or sign-in is needed: open the app, tap Listen and play any song from another device or speaker.

Microphone: audio is analysed on the device by our own engine and is never recorded, stored or uploaded. Song ID (ShazamKit) sends only an audio signature to Apple's Shazam service.

Background audio (UIBackgroundModes audio): used only when the user turns on "Keep listening in the background" (Practice tab, off by default) and has started a Listening session. A DJ can then lock the phone at the booth and keep live key/BPM detection running. The session is always shown by a Live Activity and the system microphone indicator, and can be stopped from the Live Activity, Control Center or the app. Starting from Control Center / Siri uses AudioRecordingIntent with the Live Activity, as required.

Apple Watch: independent watch app with its own microphone and on-device engine (background audio mode only while the user listens on the watch), or a mirror of the iPhone over WatchConnectivity.

Optional features that need the user's own hardware: MIDI clock out (USB / network / Bluetooth MIDI device) and "Library" (the user's own self-hosted MixAI server, found on the local network). Neither is needed to review the core features.

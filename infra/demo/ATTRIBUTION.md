# MixAI demo library — credits and licences

Every file on this demo server is published under a free licence or is in the public domain. Files are
served exactly as published by their authors (no re-encoding, no edits). The two "live" demo channels
are stream copies (`ffmpeg -c copy`) of *Big Buck Bunny* and *Sintel*, looped.
